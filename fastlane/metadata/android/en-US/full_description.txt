Falco manages your Hetzner infrastructure from your phone or tablet: Hetzner Cloud, DNS, Storage Boxes, Object Storage and dedicated servers through Robot, all in one app.

Cloud
• Servers: create, power, reboot, rebuild, rescue, ISO, resize, backups, snapshots, console, metrics and protection
• Volumes, images, placement groups and SSH keys
• Networks with subnets and routes, firewalls, floating and primary IPs with reverse DNS, load balancers and certificates
• DNS zones and records, zone file import and export
• Activity per project and per resource

Storage
• Storage Boxes: snapshots and snapshot plans, subaccounts, folders, protection
• Object Storage in every location: buckets, browsing, uploads and downloads in the background, share links, versioning

Robot
• Dedicated servers with reset, rescue, Wake on LAN, reverse DNS, failover IPs, vSwitches, SSH keys, traffic and cancellation

Made for admins
• Several accounts and Cloud projects, always visible which one you are working in, or all projects at once
• Search across every resource and project
• Phone and tablet layouts, light and dark, screen reader support

Privacy and security
• No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no third party services
• Your tokens and passwords stay on your device, in the Keychain or Android Keystore, behind Face ID, fingerprint or device PIN
• Falco talks only to the Hetzner APIs, with certificate pinning for every host
• Free software under GPL 3.0 or later
